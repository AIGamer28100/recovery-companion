Someone to talk to, right when the urge hits.

A real-time voice companion for people navigating substance use urges, and for the caregivers supporting them. Zero-typing by design, for the moments when cognitive load is highest and typing is the last thing someone can manage. Named for Soter, the Greek god of safety and deliverance from harm.

Early access. Soter Recovery is not yet published on the Play Store — the Android app below is a direct-install build for early testers. Expect rough edges, and please don't rely on it as your only safety plan yet. See Terms for what that means.
If you're in immediate danger, this app is not a substitute for emergency help. Call your local emergency number, or a crisis line (in the US: call or text 988) right now. Soter Recovery is a supportive tool, not a medical device, a crisis service, or a replacement for professional care.

Talk, don't type

A live spoken conversation is the primary interface — grounded in motivational interviewing, not a lecture.

Your caregiver, if you choose

You nominate who sees your activity. Nobody can attach themselves to you without your invitation.

Built on real recovery science

Stages of Change, harm reduction, trauma-informed care — not generic wellness-app advice.

Honest about its limits

No medication advice, ever. Points you to real emergency help when it matters more than anything the app can say.
