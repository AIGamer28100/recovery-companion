Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026 — drafted in good faith to accurately describe the app as it exists today, pending review by a licensed privacy attorney before any public launch. If you notice a mismatch between this document and the app's actual behavior, please open an issue on GitHub.

Soter Recovery is currently an early-access, pre-launch build. This policy will be reviewed and updated before any wider release.

What we collect, and why

Account information

You sign in with Google. We receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google to create your account. We never see or store your Google password.

Your role and profile

You tell us whether you're a person in recovery or a caregiver. If you're a person in recovery, you may add an emergency contact's name and phone number, and nominate a caregiver's email address so they can support you — that nomination is entirely your choice, and a caregiver has no way to attach themselves to your account without it.

Activity and check-ins

When you use a grounding tool or check in on your mood, we record the type of activity, your mood if you shared one, and the time. If you've nominated a caregiver, they can see this activity stream.

Live conversations

When you talk to the companion, your voice is streamed live to Google's Gemini API to generate a spoken response — we do not record or store the audio itself. A text transcript of the conversation is saved to your account so the companion can remember earlier context; this transcript is visible only to you, never to a caregiver.

Camera, only during a live conversation

If you turn your camera on during a conversation, video frames are sent live to Gemini so the companion can reference real things in your space. Frames are not saved as a matter of course. The one exception: if the companion detects you may be about to use, or that you have used, it saves a single snapshot image as part of an incident record (below) — this is disclosed to you immediately, out loud, every time it happens. It is never silent.

Incident records

If the companion detects a possible relapse risk during a camera session, it saves a record containing what it observed, a single snapshot image, and the time. If your caregiver is nominated, they can see this record too — you are always told when this happens, in the moment, by the companion itself.

Who can see your data

  • You can always see everything in your own account.
  • Your nominated caregiver, if you have one, can see your activity stream, incident records, and alerts generated for them — never your private conversation transcripts, and never your account settings.
  • Google processes your sign-in and hosts our infrastructure (Firebase: authentication, database, and AI Logic, which relays conversations to the Gemini API). Google does not use this data to train models or for advertising, under the terms of Firebase's data processing agreement.
  • Nobody else. We don't sell data, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything beyond running the app.

How long we keep it, stated plainly

Being direct about where this stands today rather than promising something not yet built: incident records, activity, and conversation transcripts are currently retained indefinitely and cannot be deleted by anyone, including you or us, once created — this is a deliberate anti-tampering design so a record can't be quietly erased by either side of a caregiver relationship. We know that cuts against your ability to have a single moment permanently removed, and we're not pretending otherwise. An automatic 180-day expiry for this data is designed and planned, but is not yet active as of this writing.

Deleting your account

A full account-deletion flow with a 30-day cooling-off period is designed (see the project's public design documentation) but not yet built into the app's Settings screen. Until it ships, contact us directly (see below) to request deletion of your account and associated data, and we will process that request manually.

Children's privacy

Soter Recovery is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Security

Access to your data is enforced by Firebase security rules, not just application logic — your data is scoped to your own account and anyone you've explicitly nominated, at the database level. No Gemini API key or other secret is ever present in the app itself.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters to how your data is handled, we'll update the date above and, once the app supports it, notify you in-app.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or concerns: open an issue on GitHub, or reach the developer directly through the contact information on that repository.

This app is not a crisis service. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line (in the US: call or text 988) directly.
